Frequently asked questions

What does the score mean?

Each activity gets a score from 0 to 100: 90 and up is Excellent, 75–89 Recommended, 60–74 Caution, 40–59 Poor, and below 40 Not Recommended. The score weighs the conditions that matter for that activity (wind matters a lot for golf, much less for hiking) and is capped when something is unsafe, such as lightning. It is guidance, not a guarantee. Always use your own judgment and follow official warnings.

What is the confidence percentage?

It shows how sure the app is about the forecast behind a score. Confidence falls the further ahead you look, when rain chances are borderline, when it is gusty, and when some data is missing. A score for this afternoon is far more reliable than one for next Friday.

Why does it say it is dark out?

Some activities need daylight. When the weather is fine but the sun is down, the app says so instead of calling the weather bad, and suggests the next good time.

How do I pick a place or save locations?

Tap the place name under "What should you do?" at the top of the main screen. You can use your current location or search for a city and save up to 15 places.

How do I choose which activities I see?

Open the activity picker (the sliders button at the top right) and turn activities on or off. Seasonal activities are marked, and you can still turn on out-of-season ones.

How do I turn on notifications?

Open ••• → Settings, turn on notifications, and choose which activities may notify you. Notifications are off by default. If you previously declined permission, enable them in the iOS Settings app under Weather Verdict → Notifications.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen (or Lock Screen), tap the add button, and search for Weather Verdict. The widget shows your best bet for today and updates when the app refreshes.

The widget or notifications look out of date.

They use the last forecast the app downloaded. Open the app to refresh. iOS decides how often apps may refresh in the background, so the timing varies. Make sure Background App Refresh is on in iOS Settings.

Does it work offline?

Yes. If you have no connection, the app shows your last saved forecast and tells you how old it is ("Updated 10 min ago").

Why are there no weather alerts for my country?

Official watches and warnings come from the US National Weather Service, so they appear only for US locations. Scores still account for forecast thunderstorms, strong wind, and air quality everywhere.

A score doesn't match what I'm seeing outside.

Forecasts are never perfect. On the verdict screen, tap the thumbs up or down under "Was this right?". After enough ratings the app adjusts scores to your own tolerance. You can also send me the details in an issue, including the place, activity, time and what the app said.

Can I use metric units?

Yes. Open ••• → Settings and choose Metric.

How do I delete my data?

Weather Verdict has no account and keeps everything on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
