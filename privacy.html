Privacy Policy

Weather Verdict · Last updated: November 17, 2026

The short version: Weather Verdict has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I do not collect, store or sell any of your personal information. The app needs your location only to fetch a forecast.

Who I am

Weather Verdict is published by Tom McInerney, an individual developer (“I”). You can contact me through the support page issue tracker.

Information the app uses

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location while you are using it to get the forecast for where you are. You can instead search for a place and use the app without sharing your location at all. Your coordinates are sent to the weather services listed below to retrieve a forecast. I never receive them.

Places you save and search for

Text you type into place search is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find matching cities. Places you save (up to 15) are stored only on your device.

Settings, ratings and plans

Your chosen activities, units, notification choices, “Was this right?” ratings, planned events and the last downloaded forecasts are stored only on your device. Ratings are used on your device to adjust your own scores. If you choose to export them from Settings, you decide where the file goes.

Calendar

If you tap “Add best window to Calendar,” the app asks for permission to add events. This is write-only access: the app cannot read your existing calendar events.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and off by default. They are scheduled and delivered locally on your device from the forecast it has already downloaded. No notification service on my side is involved.

Widget

The widget shows information the app has already saved on your device, shared with the widget through a private app group on your device.

Third-party services

To work, the app requests data from these services over the internet. They receive the coordinates or place text for your request, and, like any web service, they can see your device's IP address. I have no accounts with them and send them no identifiers beyond that.

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for weather forecasts, air quality and place search.
  • US National Weather Service (weather.gov) for official watches and warnings, requested only for locations in the United States.

These services have their own privacy practices, which I do not control.

What I do not do

  • I do not collect your data on any server of mine. I do not run one.
  • I do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and the app includes no third-party SDKs.
  • I do not sell or share personal information.
  • I do not require an account.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes that data. Because I hold none of it, there is nothing for me to delete on my side.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If I change how the app handles information, I will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue and I will respond.
